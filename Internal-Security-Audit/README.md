# Internal Security Audit: Botium Toys

## Scenario
Conducted an internal security audit for **Botium Toys**, a fictional U.S. based toy company expanding its global online presence. 

## Objectives
* Implementing the National Institute of Standards and Technology Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF).
* Ensuring adherence to regulations for online payment processing and E.U. operations.
* Identifying and mitigating potential vulnerabilities to critical assets.

## Task
Reviewed the IT manager’s established **scope, goals, and risk assessment report**, and completed a **controls and compliance checklist** to evaluate the company's current security posture and potential risks.
